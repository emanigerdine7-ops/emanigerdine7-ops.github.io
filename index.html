    The "Benito Bowl" Was a Statement, Not a Translation

    February 9, 2026

    Last night’s Super Bowl LX halftime show was a masterpiece of representation. While the hate on social media focuses on the language barrier, they are missing the point. Bad Bunny did exactly what Kendrick Lamar did last year—he used the world’s biggest stage to represent his people with zero apologies.

    The most emotional moment was the tribute to Liam Conejo Ramos. By handing his actual Grammy to a young boy on stage (representing the 5-year-old recently detained by ICE), Benito made a powerful political statement that didn't need a single word of English to be understood.

    The show was about the vibe, the energy, and the culture. From the domino players to the salsa remixes, it was a party for everyone. If you couldn't understand the lyrics, there were captions, but the real goal was to dance and vibe. Music is universal, and Benito proved you don't need to speak English to command the room.

    The hate isn't deserved—it was a great show for people of Spanish descent and for all of us.

    When the Homework Gets Hard: Using AI as a 24/7 Tutor

    February 11, 2026

    We’ve all been there—it’s late at night, you’re staring at a hard assignment, and you’re stuck on a concept that just won't click. Whether it’s trying to calculate z-scores and variance in Statistics, or figuring out macronutrient percentages for a Nutrition project, sometimes you just need a nudge in the right direction.

    That is where AI really shines as a resource. It isn’t about getting the answer; it’s about having a tutor that never sleeps and can explain things in five different ways until you actually get it.

    How AI Helps Me Breakthrough:

    • Step-by-Step Breakdown: When a Kinesiology definition like "Biomechanics" feels too abstract, I can ask AI to explain it using a real-world sports example.
    • Checking the Logic: In Statistics, I can explain how I got to a specific median or standard deviation, and the AI can point out exactly where my math went off track.
    • Simplifying the Science: Nutrition can get heavy with technical terms. AI helps by "translating" complex biological processes into something easier to study for an exam.

    The key is to use it as a tutor, not a shortcut. If you ask it "How do I solve this?" instead of "Give me the answer," you actually learn the material. It turns a frustrating night of homework into a study session where you actually feel prepared for the next test.

    How are you guys using AI to get through your hardest classes this semester? Let’s share some tips in the comments!

    Redesigning the Dorm: Why We Need Houses, Not Just Beds

    February 11, 2026

    College is supposed to be our home for four years, but right now, most dorms feel more like a temporary room than a real place to live. I think schools, including Lindenwood, should rethink how they build student housing. We need spaces that feel like houses, not just a bed and a bathroom.

    Access to Real Food

    People always talk about how college students don’t eat enough, but look at what we have access to. Most of us are stuck with a microwave and processed foods that we are getting tired of. We need kitchens. Even if it’s not in every individual room, every student lounge area should have a full kitchen. Being able to cook a real meal would change everything for our health and our energy levels.

    The Support of Pets

    Being far from home and not knowing many people in a new place is hard on your mental health. I love my dog and wish he could be here with me without the stress of him having to be a "certified" support animal. Not every pet is a certified service animal, but they all provide support. If you're struggling with being away from home, having your pet there makes a massive difference, and you shouldn't have to pay extra for that companionship.

    The Need for Privacy

    Finally, we need to talk about roommates. Everyone is different, and not everyone respects your sleep or your privacy. The suite-mate idea is okay, but sharing a single room is tough. You can't truly be in your own space or have your boyfriend or girlfriend over comfortably when you have a roommate there. We deserve our own privacy and a space where we can actually do what we want.

    College is a big transition. Making dorms feel more like actual homes would make that transition so much easier for everyone.
